# v1.60: Settings rebuilt around three parts

Settings now has three tabs: **Home screen**, **Lock screen**, **App drawer**. Controls were moved, not copied, so every saved value, id and handler is unchanged.

- Home screen: Clock (Style & pill | Dial | Time & position), Mandala, Search bar, Music bar, Dock (Dock | Glow & glass), Weather, Date bar, Widgets & pages (Advanced).
- Lock screen: Lock screen (on/off, delay, dim, unlock hint), Clock, Music bar, Weather, Date bar, Feature cards (Advanced). The lock screen has no mandala, search bar or dock, so those are not listed.
- App drawer: Style (Layout | Arc | Order; Arc includes the arc themes), Apps (Apps | Icons & grid), Contacts, then Settings: Themes & Look (packs, colours, time of day, wallpaper), Display & accessibility, Permissions & system, and under Advanced Gestures, Effects, Power & privacy, Focus timer, Tasks, Quick actions, Launcher, Developer, About.
- Simple / Advanced switch at the top. Simple hides the sections and sub-tabs marked Advanced.
- Hold-to-open on the home screen jumps to the matching section (the date card now opens Date bar, the clock pill opens Clock > Style & pill).
- Changed: `app/src/web/index.src.html` (new `v160-settings-rework` script at the end, small hooks in hold-to-open and live preview) and generated `assets/index.html`; `APP_VER` / `versionName` 1.60. No Kotlin change.
