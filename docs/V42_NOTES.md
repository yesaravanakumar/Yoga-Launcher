# v42 changes (app drawer)

1. **Smarter search** - ranks exact, starts-with, word-start, initials (gp = Google Pay), contains, then loose letters (whtsp = WhatsApp) and package name. Often-used apps win ties. The best match is first on the arc and Enter opens it (before, results were re-sorted A to Z).
2. **Suggested chip** - apps you usually open around this hour (+/- 1 hour). Appears after you have launched a few apps. "Clear recents" also clears it.
3. **Collapse on close** - the arc rolls back in (same Opening animation setting; off = instant).
4. **Tap a side app to roll it to the centre** (Themes & Look > Arcs, off by default). Tap the centred app to open it.
5. **Tap the "n / total" counter** to jump to the first app, tap again for the last.

New setting key: dtap. New storage key: mdc-hr.
