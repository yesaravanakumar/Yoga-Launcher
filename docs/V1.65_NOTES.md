# v1.65: Reorder and Select in every drawer layout, no wasted space

## What changed
- **Reorder now works in Grid, List, Smart, Pages, Crescent and Arc Wave** (before: only Grid and List, plus Arc Wave's own).
  - Grid / List: hold an app, drag it (as before).
  - Smart: while reordering, the Favourites / Suggested / Recent rows are hidden so no app shows twice. Drag inside a group.
  - Pages: drag inside a page, or hold the app at the left/right edge to flip the page, then drop it on an app of the other page.
  - Crescent: the app in the centre moves with Up / Down.
  - Arc Wave: unchanged (Up / Down, or drag the big icon).
- **Select works in every layout** (Grid, List, Smart, Pages, Crescent, Arc Wave) and the flat bar now has All and Home like Arc Wave.
- **Fixed a swap-back bug**: after an app swapped places, the neighbour was still sliding under the finger and swapped straight back, so drags often did nothing.
- **Reordering a block only moves apps in that block** (the grid, one Smart group, one page); apps elsewhere keep their place.

## Space
- Flat layouts and Crescent: Reorder / Select sit in one slim strip in the empty band above the search box. Select turns into a single-row bar there (count, All, Home, Pin, Hide, trash, Done). The old bottom bar over the apps is gone, and so are the "hold an app..." toasts (a short hint shows in the strip instead).
- Arc Wave: the Select bar is one row (was two), buttons are smaller, uninstall is a trash icon, no toasts over the wave; Reorder shows a one-line hint.

Changed: `app/src/web/index.src.html` + generated `assets/index.html`; version 1.65. No Kotlin change.
Checked in headless Chromium (390x844) with 44 test apps: drag reorder (Grid, List, Smart, Pages incl. across pages), Crescent Up/Down, Arc Wave Up/Down, select + hide in all six layouts, no page errors. Not yet tried with a finger on a real phone.
