Yoga Launcher: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 October 2026 · App: Yoga Launcher (com.yoga.launcher) · Developer: [DEVELOPER NAME] · Contact: [CONTACT EMAIL]

Yoga Launcher is an Android home-screen launcher. It runs on your phone and has no account, no sign-in and no developer-run server. We do not collect, store or sell your personal data. This page explains what the app touches on your device and the few things it sends to third-party services.

What stays on your device

Your settings, theme and pill/clock choices, favourites, home layout, tasks and notes are stored only on your device. If Android backup is on for your device, Android may include these app settings in your own Google backup; that is controlled by you in Android settings, not by us.

You can turn any of these off at any time in Android Settings. The launcher keeps working without them, with the matching feature switched off.

Services the app contacts

  • Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com, geocoding-api.open-meteo.com): receives coordinates, or a city name you type, to return the forecast.
  • BigDataCloud (api.bigdatacloud.net): receives coordinates to return the nearest place name.
  • Search suggestions (suggestqueries.google.com): receives the text you type in the launcher search box, only while you type, to offer suggestions. Turn suggestions off in Settings to stop this.
  • Web links and search: when you run a web search or open a link, it opens in your browser or the chosen app, which then applies its own privacy policy.

These services receive your device's IP address as part of any internet request and handle it under their own policies. The app has no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting libraries.

Other apps sending cards to the launcher

Only apps signed with the same key as Yoga Launcher can send it cards. Other apps cannot.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Your choices and deleting data

Clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app, to delete everything it holds. Because we do not hold your data on any server, there is nothing further for us to delete.

Changes

If the app starts using data in a new way, this page will be updated and the effective date changed before the release that does so.
