# v1.48 (velocity stretch + speed-aware detents)

All in `app/src/main/assets/index.html` plus one small method in `YogaBridge.kt`. Not yet tested on a phone.

- **Velocity stretch**: icons stretch along the arc's tangent in proportion to how fast the wheel turns (max +28 %, a little squash across), then relax as it settles (~0.15 s). Speed is measured from the wheel position itself (`st.sv`, apps per second), so it works for drag, fling, spring, A-Z scrub, wheel and keys. Only runs in Motion effects = Full, outside Performance mode and reduced-motion. New setting `dsq` (Themes & Look > Arcs: "Icons stretch along the arc when you fling"). Whisper preset turns it off.
- **Detent haptics**: one click per app passing the focus lens, tuned to speed. Slow turn = full click, fast = light tick, very fast = ticks thinned to one per ~42 ms so they stay separate. A heavy click when a fling hits the end of the list. Replaces the old fixed tick (`dhp`, now labelled "Haptic detents").
- **Native haptics**: new `Android.haptic(kind)` in `YogaBridge.kt` plays Android's own TICK / CLICK / HEAVY_CLICK effects (Android 10+). If it is missing or returns false, the page falls back to `navigator.vibrate` with short pulses.
- The A-Z scrub still gives its stronger group tick; the detent skips that step so the two do not cancel each other.
