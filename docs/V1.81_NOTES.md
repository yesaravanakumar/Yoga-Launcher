# v1.81: four curved drawers on one engine
Pick one in Settings > App drawer > Drawer layout: Thumb arc (default), Orbit dial, Wave ribbon, Fan stack. Grid, List, Smart and Pages are unchanged. Old Arc and Crescent choices open Thumb arc.

- One engine: the list, fling and snap are shared; each design only says where an app at distance r from the selected one sits (slotD).
- Selected app has Pin, Add to home and More (More opens the options menu with info, rename, Play Store, uninstall). Search and Contacts are the two round buttons top left; Arc settings gear is bottom left.
- A-Z strip is a straight strip on the right edge (left edge for Fan stack). The selected name shrinks to fit (min 13px), then ends with "...".
- Motion: open 0.25s, 20ms stagger, snap 20, selected pop 0.2s; apps shrink to 85% and fade to 40% with distance; no blur; idle visuals are static and decorative lines rest while scrolling.
- Arc settings panel trimmed to: line position, app spacing, icon size, name size, names, A-Z, haptics, tools, badges.
Not done: category text next to the name in Orbit dial; Wave ribbon "bend" is a small sideways nudge of the line, not a true curve bend; Orbit search stays at the top with the other designs.
