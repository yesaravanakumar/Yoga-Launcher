# v1.56 notes: sort, reorder and select in the arc, Crescent back in settings, wrapped contact names

- **Arc Wave now has Sort, Reorder and Select** (three small pills at the top right of the arc drawer).
  - **Sort:** A to Z, Most used, Newest, Custom order. Same setting as the Grid drawer (`mdc-xsort`).
  - **Reorder:** tap Reorder, then drag the big selected icon up or down, or use the Up / Down buttons. The order is the same custom order the Grid drawer uses (`mdc-xord`), so the two stay in step. It needs an empty search and the apps view.
  - **Select:** tap apps (the wave icons or the big one) to tick them. The bar at the top offers All, Home (add to home screen), Hide, Uninstall and Done. Hide uses the same hidden list as the app menu, so Settings > Unhide all still works.
  - The three pills are not shown in Contacts. They can be switched off in Arc settings > Look > "Sort, Reorder and Select buttons".
- **Drawer layout (Settings > App drawer):** Arc Wave, **Crescent** (the original curved wheel, brought back), Grid, List, Smart, Pages. Saved in `yoga-dlay` (`arc` | `crescent` | `grid`) and `mdc-dstl`. `window.drawerLayout.set('arc'|'crescent'|'grid'|'list'|'smart'|'pages')`. The "Use Crescent" button in Arc themes now switches the layout too.
- **Contacts arc:** names wrap (up to 3 lines for the selected contact, 2 for the others) instead of running over the A-Z letters and the next contact. The contact action buttons (call, WhatsApp, message, SMS) are kept inside the screen and clear of the next contact.
- **Search:** the square outline that appeared around the typed text (search overlay, arc search, drawer and settings search) is gone. The rounded glass bar still lights up when focused.
- Changed: `app/src/web/index.src.html` and generated `assets/index.html`; `APP_VER` and `versionName` now 1.56. No Kotlin change.
- Checked in headless Chromium (390x844): sort menu, reorder by arrows and by drag (order saved), select with Home, Hide, All and Uninstall (no bridge message), contacts with long names, every layout switch, layout saved across reload, search outline off. No page errors. The jsdom smoke tests were not run (dependencies are not installed here).
- Fix: bulk "Home" from the arc now turns on "Apps on home screen" and places each app on a free spot (same as the single Add to home button), so they actually show on the home screen.
- Fix (bug check): apps hidden from the arc (app menu or bulk Hide) came back the next time the arc opened. The arc list now always leaves hidden apps out; typing a hidden app's exact name still finds it.
- Fix (bug check): Reorder does nothing while a search is typed (it could shuffle only the matches); a short message says to clear the search first.

## Settings: one App drawer section with tabs
- **Settings > App drawer** now holds everything about the drawer, in five tabs:
  - **Layout:** drawer layout (Arc Wave, Crescent, Grid, List, Smart, Pages), categories, A-Z index and bend, loop list, motion effects, touch controls.
  - **Arc:** Minimalistic drawer, All arc options, a shortcut to Arc themes, colours and shape, and switches for the Sort / Reorder / Select buttons and for notification dots and NEW badges on the arc. A note shows when the layout is not Arc Wave.
  - **Order:** sort apps, recents, reset custom order.
  - **Apps:** badge settings (moved here from At a glance & notifications and Apps & packages), a shortcut to Icon style, size and grid, and Unhide all apps (moved here from Search).
  - **Search:** the whole former Search section (home search bar, engine, suggestions, history).
- The old **Search** and **Apps & packages** sections are gone (their controls moved, none were removed). Holding the home search bar opens App drawer on the Search tab; holding the arc opens it on the Arc tab. Searching in settings shows every tab at once. App drawer also shows in the Simple view. The last tab used is remembered (`yoga-dtab`).
- One "Reset this section" at the bottom resets everything in App drawer.
