# v1.52 notes: preview, music, search, Business check

- **Show preview**: after Hide then Show preview, the Settings home-preview card stayed on "Home preview hidden". Fixed (the redraw cache is cleared when hidden).
- **Music removed**: the music player above the dock, its settings (Music player, Player style, Progress line), and the "Music: on/off" line and bar in the preview card. The "Now playing" widget and the lock-screen now-playing line are untouched. Backend (`getMedia`, `mediaControl`) is untouched.
- **Search**: pressing Enter right after typing in the home search used the previous result list (e.g. launched the wrong app). Enter and arrow keys now rank the typed text first.
- **Business mode**: checked, not changed. `sync()` in `yoga-biz-js` has `on=false` (v44 switched it off), so nothing can show the panel. The panel itself renders and its tiles launch apps / fall back to websites when forced on.
- Tests: `tools/smoke/check.js` and `check2.js` added.
- **Contact photos in the Arc drawer (Contacts view)**: `getContacts()` now also returns each contact's photo address (`u`), and the new `getContactPhoto(uri)` returns a 128 px JPEG data URI of the picture stored on the phone. The page loads photos one at a time, caches them, and shows the initial letter until a photo arrives. Contacts without a photo keep the letter. No internet lookup (see reply). Kotlin change: needs a rebuild; not compiled here.
- **Contact quick actions in the Arc drawer**: with a contact selected, three small buttons appear under the name: Call (opens the dialer), WhatsApp message, WhatsApp call. New Kotlin bridge calls `waChat(number)` and `waCall(number)` (no WhatsApp data is read; the call button uses the contact's WhatsApp call entry in the phone's contacts and falls back to opening the chat). Numbers without a country code get the phone's country code. Needs a rebuild; Kotlin not compiled here.
- **Icons on every contact row**: each contact in the Arc drawer also shows the three small icons (Call, WhatsApp call, WhatsApp message) under its name; the selected contact keeps the larger row.
